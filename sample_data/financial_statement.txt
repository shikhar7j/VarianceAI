NORTHBRIDGE INDUSTRIES INC.
CONDENSED FINANCIAL SUMMARY (SYNTHETIC SAMPLE DATA FOR DEMO PURPOSES)
Fiscal Year 2026, Third Quarter Report

OVERVIEW
Northbridge Industries Inc. is a fictional mid-cap manufacturing and logistics company used here as
sample data for demonstration purposes only. All figures below are illustrative and not real.

REVENUE
Total revenue for Q3 2026 was $46.5 million, a decrease of 7% compared to the budgeted $50.0 million.
Management attributes the shortfall primarily to delayed shipments in the logistics division caused by
a regional customs backlog, along with softer-than-expected demand in the North American retail segment.
Revenue in the prior quarter, Q2 2026, was $48.2 million, and revenue in Q3 2025 (year-ago quarter) was
$44.1 million, representing 5.4% year-over-year growth despite the quarter-over-quarter softness.

COST OF GOODS SOLD (COGS)
COGS for Q3 2026 came in at $21.5 million against a budget of $20.0 million, a 7.5% unfavorable variance.
The increase was primarily driven by elevated raw material costs, particularly steel and specialty
polymers, which rose approximately 12% quarter-over-quarter due to global supply chain constraints.

OPERATING EXPENSES
Marketing spend was $6.1 million versus a budget of $4.0 million, a 52.5% overspend. This was driven by
an unplanned mid-quarter brand campaign launched to counter a competitor's aggressive pricing promotion.
Travel and entertainment expenses came in significantly under budget at $42,000 versus $80,000 budgeted,
reflecting continued organizational preference for virtual client meetings.
Professional services costs were $135,000 against a budget of $90,000, a 50% overspend, attributed to
one-time legal and advisory fees related to a vendor contract renegotiation.

HEADCOUNT AND SALARIES
Salaries and wages totaled $1.18 million, slightly under the $1.2 million budget, reflecting two open
engineering positions that remained unfilled through the quarter.

OUTLOOK
Management expects the customs-related shipment delays to resolve by early Q4 2026 and has flagged raw
material cost inflation as an ongoing risk to monitor for the remainder of the fiscal year. The board has
approved an incremental $2 million contingency budget for Q4 to address potential further supply chain
disruption.

RISK FACTORS
Key risks disclosed for the upcoming quarter include: continued volatility in steel and polymer input
costs, potential further customs delays affecting the logistics division, and competitive pricing
pressure in the North American retail segment that may require sustained elevated marketing investment.
